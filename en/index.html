How to organize a folder for import
Option 1 — a single exam. Folder = deck, subfolders = composers:
📁 Winter exam, year 1 ← deck 📁 Bach ← composer 🎵 Prelude.mp3 📁 Mozart ← composer 🎵 Sonata.mp3
Option 2 — all exams at once (set up once). Pick a parent folder that contains deck folders:
📁 My exams ← pick THIS folder 📁 Winter exam ← deck 📁 Bach ← composer 🎵 Prelude.mp3 📁 Summer exam ← deck 📁 Chopin ← composer 🎵 Nocturne.mp3
The app detects the structure and creates all decks. Switch decks at the top (▾). Later, hit “Sync with folder” and pick the same folder — the app synchronizes: new files are added, changed ones updated, files removed from the folder are removed too (it asks before deleting).

MusQuiz — online drop-the-needle trainer

MusQuiz helps you prepare for music history and listening exams: you hear excerpts and learn to recognize the composer and the piece by ear. It works for college music majors, conservatory students, music appreciation classes — and anyone who loves classical music.

How practice works

Upload audio files or a whole folder with your exam syllabus — the app builds the set automatically: the deck takes the folder name and composers come from subfolders. Four modes: Learning with notes and hints, Practice on random excerpts, Exam with a final grade, and Drill that repeats your mistakes until every track sticks.

Why it works

The quiz runs right in your browser, completely free: no accounts, your audio stays on your device and is never uploaded. The site installs as an app on phones and computers and works offline. Progress, per-composer stats and notes save automatically, and you can send a whole deck to classmates as a single file.

Regular drop-the-needle practice trains your ear and aural analysis: you start recognizing a composer’s style, timbres and themes faster — exactly what listening exams test.

FAQ

Is it free? Yes — completely free, no ads.

What repertoire can I practice? Anything — from Bach and Mozart to Prokofiev and Shostakovich: you upload your own syllabus.

Will it help me prepare for an exam? Yes: Exam mode mimics the real test — random excerpts, no hints, no seeking, graded at the end.

Install the app

MusQuiz installs like a regular app: an icon on your screen, its own window, works offline.

💻
Computer (Chrome / Edge)
The install icon at the right of the address bar, or menu ⋮ → “Install app”.
🤖
Android (Chrome)
Menu ⋮ (three dots) → “Install app” / “Add to Home screen”.
🍏
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
The Share button (square with an arrow ⬆️) → “Add to Home Screen”.
Note: the installed app has its own storage — move tracks and progress via backup (⚙ → “Save to file”, then “Restore” inside the app).
